Rent or toxicity? A replay of the market-making wargame

Every frame is a recorded period of the simulator used in the paper. One period is one taker arriving. A tick is the smallest price step; quotes are half-spreads measured in ticks from the mid price.

Regenerate the recordings with python experiments/trace.py --all. The taker's type (informed or not) is never public in the model; it is shown here only after the fill, to explain what happened. Marks (what a fill turned out to be worth) become public after the mark lag. A replay is one short run and its profits are noisy; the numbers in the paper are averages over many cartels and seeds. In the identity and one-tick scenarios the entrant has already watched the tape for 3000 periods when the replay starts.
